## Step 1: research

Open every evidence URL above, then the company's own site: product pages,
careers page, blog, and public founder posts. Do not use LinkedIn. Work out:
- what they sell and to whom;
- which team would feel this problem (ops, support, growth, sales, the founders);
- what that team does by hand today, as far as public pages show;
- whether my guess holds up. If it does not, say so and name the better problem
  the sources point to, with the quote.

Keep facts (with URL) apart from your inferences.

## Step 2: three options

Propose three different prototypes for that problem. For each, give:
- who at {company_name} would use it, and the moment they would open it;
- what it does, in two sentences;
- why a founder would care this week, tied to a quote above;
- how big it is: 2-3 hours, half a day, or a full day;
- the risk that it misses (for example, "they may already have a tool for this").

Rules for every option: an internal tool for their own team, never a copy or
cheaper version of what they sell; it works on sample data, labelled as such;
it can be understood from one screen in under a minute; no paid APIs, keys or
login; and it deploys free (Vercel or GitHub Pages).

Recommend one, say why, then stop and wait for me to choose.

## Build the prototype

After I choose, write the build plan for that option:
- screens or flow, step by step;
- the sample data (fields and about 10-30 realistic rows, marked as sample);
- file layout and stack (a single Next.js app or plain HTML/CSS/JS);
- the edge cases worth handling;
- a 60-90 second demo script I can record.

Then help me build it step by step. Run it after each step.

## Acceptance criteria

- It runs with one command, written at the top of the README.
- A founder understands what it does from the first screen.
- The README has "What I assumed" and "Sample data" sections.
- Nothing in it states a fact about {company_name} that is not in the sources.

When it is done, give me exactly this block for my cold email:

PROTOTYPE_LINK: the live URL
WHAT_IT_DOES: one plain sentence a founder would understand
WHAT_I_ASSUMED: one sentence naming the biggest assumption
